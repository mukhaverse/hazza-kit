# About Me (by Hazza)

Welcome to my world!!!

My name is Hazza Sherbetli and I was born on a rainy day in Jeddah, which is very rare, so my mother says that's why I am special. Since I was a child I was always curious about how things work. When I was 7 years old I opened our TV remote to see what is inside and I could not close it again, and that was the moment I knew I wanted to be an engineer (my father did not agree it was a good moment).

In middle school I discovered computers. I played a lot of games and then I thought: why play games when I can make them? So I started learning HTML from YouTube. My first website was about my favourite cartoon and it had 14 pages and a guestbook. Nobody signed it except my cousin.

In high school I was the "tech guy" in my family. Every time the WiFi stopped, they called me. Every time my aunt's phone was full, they called me. I fixed 400+ problems (I did not count, but probably).

Now I am a third-year Software Engineering student. I am a passionate, hardworking, creative, detail-oriented team player and a fast learner who loves technology and solving problems. I believe that coding is not just a job, it is a lifestyle. My motto is "Dream it. Code it. Ship it." I am always eager to learn new things and take on new challenges in the world of programming and development.

I live with my grandmother, Teta Noura, and she is the reason I built my best app (see Jaddati). She is my first user and my toughest tester. If Teta can't use it, it's not done. I also have a cat called Null. I found her on an empty street, so the name was obvious. She sleeps on my keyboard and sometimes she commits code.

I drink 4 cups of karak every day from Abu Fares's stand near the engineering building. Actually that is how Karak Queue started: I was late to a lecture because of the line. Twice. OK, many times.

In my free time I like building side projects (I have 47 repositories on GitHub), watching tech videos, and trying new restaurants in Jeddah with my friends.

I am looking for an internship where I can learn, grow, and make an impact. Thank you for reading my story until the end!!! You are a legend.
